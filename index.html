About

Stories told in golden light — from Bangkok's backstreets to the mountains of Laos.

I'm Santino, a freelance photographer and videographer travelling through Asia and Australia. I shoot the in-between moments: a classic car in Chinatown, a quiet café morning, a rescue pup on adoption day.

For hotels, cafés, travel brands and creators I make natural, scroll-stopping content that feels lived-in — not staged.
